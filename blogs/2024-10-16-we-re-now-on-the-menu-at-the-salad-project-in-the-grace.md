You can find our Superstraccia in the Grace Beverley Bowl in all Salad Project locations. They have partnered with @gracebeverley to bring you a bowl designed to power performance on busy days, inspired by Grace's need for lasting energy and focus, as the busiest woman we know. The Grace Beverley Bowl is a plant-based twist on their much-loved salad, The GOAT, featuring maple-tamari mushrooms, JULIENNE BRUNO Superstraccia, and red rice.
